NewsHub – Three targeted improvements

- FactCheck: claim-specific evidence relevance, contextual negation handling, independent-publisher counting, syndicated-copy discounting, Mixed verdict for relevant conflicts, and stricter insufficient-evidence behaviour.
- NewsLens: same-event matching based on event terms, title/context similarity, entities and time proximity; category equality alone is excluded.
- TruthTrace: stricter claim-to-article matching and stale false-positive cleanup within the evaluated window.
- Categorisation: generic World feed articles can be refined using high-specificity topic rules; manual/editor overrides and explicit publisher sections are preserved.
- Added regression tests for FactCheck, NewsLens/TruthTrace matching and categorisation.

No schema migration is required.
